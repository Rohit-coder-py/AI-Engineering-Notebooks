## 📌 About This Repository

<p>
This repo is my <b>public learning log</b> on the road to becoming an <b>AI Engineer</b> — not a course, not a tutorial, but a real, evolving record of what I study, build, break, and fix along the way.
</p>

<p>
It is <ins>intentionally unfinished</ins>. As I explore new libraries, frameworks, and topics, this repository keeps growing with them. Think of it less like a finished product and more like a <b>changelog of my skill-building</b>.
</p>

> 🔭 **Currently exploring:** Deep Learning (Month 4 of a 6-month self-designed roadmap) — while going back to strengthen my math foundations.
