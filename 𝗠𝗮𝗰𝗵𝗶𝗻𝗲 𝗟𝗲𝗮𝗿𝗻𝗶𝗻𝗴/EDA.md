EDA (Exploratory Data Analysis) is the process of exploring,
understanding, cleaning, and analyzing a dataset to discover its
characteristics, identify patterns, detect anomalies, and gain
meaningful insights before building machine learning models.

The main purpose of EDA is to answer questions such as:

What does the dataset contain? Are there missing values or duplicate
records? Are there outliers? How are the features distributed? Are there
relationships between variables? Which features are important? What
preprocessing steps will be required?

EDA combines statistical summaries and data visualizations to understand
the data thoroughly and make informed decisions for the next stages of
the machine learning pipeline.

Objectives of EDA Understand the structure of the dataset. Assess data
quality. Detect missing values and duplicates. Identify outliers and
anomalies. Study feature distributions. Analyze relationships between
variables. Discover trends and patterns. Generate business insights.
