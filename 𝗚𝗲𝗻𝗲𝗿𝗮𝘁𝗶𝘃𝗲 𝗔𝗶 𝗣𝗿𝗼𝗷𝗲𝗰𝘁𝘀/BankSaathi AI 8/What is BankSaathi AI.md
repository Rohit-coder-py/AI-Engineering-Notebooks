## What is BankSaathi AI?

BankSaathi AI is a multilingual AI chatbot designed to help people understand common banking topics through simple conversations. You can ask a question in **Hindi, English, or Hinglish** and get an easy-to-understand response.

Whether you want to learn what UPI means, understand a common banking term, or get general guidance about debit cards and ATM usage, BankSaathi AI aims to make banking information more approachable.

## Why use BankSaathi AI?

- **Speak naturally:** Ask questions in Hindi, English, or Hinglish.
- **Understand banking terms:** Get simple explanations instead of complicated terminology.
- **Ask follow-up questions:** Continue a conversation to clarify something you did not understand.
- **Get approachable guidance:** Explore general topics such as UPI, debit cards, ATMs, and account statements.
- **Learn at your own pace:** Ask for a simpler explanation or request a response in another supported language.

## What can you ask?

Examples of questions you could ask include:

- “What is UPI, and how does it work?”
- “UPI PIN kya hota hai?”
- “Explain the difference between a debit card and a credit card.”
- “How can I find my account statement in my bank's official app?”
- “Please explain this banking term in simple Hindi.”
- “Can you explain that again in English?”

## Designed around your needs

BankSaathi AI is intended to make general banking information easier to understand. It is designed to be conversational and accessible, especially for users who prefer Hindi or Hinglish over formal English.

The assistant can make mistakes, so important information should always be checked with your bank's official app, website, or verified customer-support channel.

## Your safety matters

**Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, ATM PIN, password, CVV, or full card details in a chat.**

BankSaathi AI is a learning project that provides general information. It does not access your bank account, check your balance, verify transactions, transfer money, block cards, or perform banking operations.

For account-specific problems, suspected fraud, or urgent issues, contact your bank through its official channels.

## Current project scope

BankSaathi AI is being developed as an AI-powered banking information assistant. Available features depend on the current version of the application; it should not be treated as an official service of any bank.
