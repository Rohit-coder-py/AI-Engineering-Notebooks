## Welcome to KrishiMitra AI

**KrishiMitra AI** is an AI-powered agricultural assistant designed to help farmers and people interested in agriculture find and understand useful farming information through a conversational interface.

Agriculture often raises practical questions about crop cultivation, soil health, irrigation, pests and diseases, farming practices, and agricultural schemes. Relevant information can be spread across documents, websites, research papers, and official resources. KrishiMitra AI aims to bring information from supported knowledge sources into one place so users can ask questions in everyday language.

## What can you use it for?

Depending on the information available in its knowledge sources, KrishiMitra AI can help you:

- **Understand farming topics** — get clear explanations of agricultural concepts and practices.
- **Explore crop-related information** — look up guidance contained in the documents and sources connected to the assistant.
- **Learn about soil and irrigation** — ask questions about information available in its reference materials.
- **Explore pest and disease information** — understand material found in the available agricultural resources.
- **Find information in documents** — ask questions about supported documents instead of manually searching every page.
- **Learn about agricultural resources and schemes** — explore information included in relevant government or other trusted sources.
- **Ask follow-up questions** — continue a conversation to clarify an answer or understand a topic better.

*The answers KrishiMitra AI can provide depend on the sources connected to the application, the quality of those sources, and the capabilities of the AI model being used. These features should not be interpreted as a guarantee that every topic or live data source is currently supported.*

## How does KrishiMitra AI work?

KrishiMitra AI is designed around **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**. RAG allows an AI assistant to look up relevant information from a knowledge collection and use it as context when answering a question.

A simplified workflow looks like this:

1. **Knowledge is added** — information may come from supported files, documents, or other configured sources.
2. **Text is extracted** — readable content is collected from the source.
3. **Content is split into chunks** — long documents are divided into smaller, searchable sections.
4. **Embeddings are created** — an embedding model converts chunks into numerical representations that help identify related content.
5. **Information is indexed** — chunks and their representations are stored in a retrieval system or vector store.
6. **A user asks a question** — KrishiMitra AI searches for relevant content.
7. **Relevant context is retrieved** — useful sections are selected from the available knowledge.
8. **The AI generates a response** — the language model uses the question and retrieved context to prepare an answer.

**In simple words:** RAG helps the assistant look for relevant information before generating an answer. It does not automatically mean that the language model has been retrained on every uploaded document.

## What kinds of knowledge can it use?

The project is intended to support agricultural knowledge from multiple kinds of sources, depending on which loaders and integrations are implemented and enabled.

| Source type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Text files | `.txt`, `.md` |
| Documents | PDF, DOCX |
| Tables and data | CSV, Excel |
| Websites | Agricultural information pages and official resources |
| Research material | Agricultural guides and research papers |
| Images and scans | Scanned pages or photographs, when OCR is configured |
| Video text | YouTube transcripts or captions, when available |
| Other sources | Manually entered text, databases, or APIs when integrations are configured |

Not every source type is necessarily available in every version of the app. Website access, OCR, video transcripts, databases, and live APIs require the relevant functionality to be implemented and configured.

## Why use KrishiMitra AI?

- **A conversational experience:** ask a question in your own words.
- **Knowledge from multiple sources:** relevant information can be collected from supported sources.
- **Less manual searching:** retrieve useful passages without reading every document from beginning to end.
- **Context-aware responses:** the assistant can use retrieved material to make an answer more relevant to the question.
- **A foundation for agricultural knowledge access:** the project can grow as additional trusted sources and integrations are added.

## Tips for getting better answers

For a more useful response:

1. **Ask a specific question.** Include the crop, topic, or problem you want to understand.
2. **Add relevant details.** Where appropriate, mention your region, crop stage, soil conditions, or symptoms.
3. **Ask one thing at a time.** Break a complicated question into smaller questions.
4. **Request clarification.** If an answer is unclear, ask the assistant to explain it in simpler terms.
5. **Check the source.** When source references are provided, review the original material and its date.
6. **Report uncertainty.** If the answer lacks enough information, provide more context rather than relying on a guess.

## Important limitations and responsible use

KrishiMitra AI is an information assistant, not a substitute for a qualified local agricultural expert.

- **AI can make mistakes.** A fluent answer is not necessarily a correct answer.
- **Knowledge may be incomplete or outdated.** The assistant can only retrieve information available to it and accessible through its configured sources.
- **Live information needs live sources.** Weather, market prices, and current scheme details can change frequently. These should be checked through appropriate, up-to-date sources or configured live services.
- **Local conditions matter.** Crop recommendations may vary by region, season, soil, climate, and crop variety.
- **Use care with high-impact decisions.** Before applying pesticides, changing treatment practices, investing money, or making decisions that could affect crops or livestock, verify advice with reliable official guidance or a qualified agricultural professional.
- **Protect personal information.** Avoid entering passwords, banking details, or other sensitive information into the assistant.

## Our goal

The goal of KrishiMitra AI is to make agricultural knowledge easier to find and understand by combining document retrieval with AI-generated explanations. As reliable sources and useful integrations are added, it can become a more helpful way for users to explore farming information.

## A note for users

KrishiMitra AI provides informational responses based on the knowledge and tools available to it. It does not guarantee crop outcomes, diagnose every plant disease, confirm eligibility for a government scheme, or replace official advice. Always verify important agricultural, financial, and government-related information with authoritative sources.
